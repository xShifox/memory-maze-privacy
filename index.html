Privacy Policy

Memory Maze · Memory Maze Studio · Last updated 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

The short version. Memory Maze has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your progress is saved in your device's app storage. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects advertising data as described below. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information ourselves, because we have no way to receive any.

Who we are

Memory Maze is published by Memory Maze Studio, an independent developer. Contact us at support@memorymaze.app.

What the app stores, and where it goes

The app saves the following in Android's standard app storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play screen
  • Your highest unlocked level and the stars earned per level
  • Your best endless-mode score and deepest level reached
  • Total levels completed and total wall collisions
  • Your sound on/off preference

Device backup. Android's backup system is enabled for this app. If you have backup or device-to-device transfer switched on in your device settings, Android may copy this app's data - including the progress listed above - to your Google Drive backup, or directly to a new device when you transfer or upgrade. In that case the data is stored and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete it from your Google account or from Android's backup settings. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes the copy on the device.

None of this data identifies you personally.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The app is supported by ads served through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Three formats are used:

Banner

Shown on menu screens only — the title, level select, pause, results and game-over screens. It is never shown while you are navigating a maze.

Interstitial

A full-screen ad is shown only at the end of a level, and only when you press a button that continues play. It is never shown while a maze is in progress, never on app start, and never when you leave the app.

  • After you run out of hearts — pressing Retry on the game-over screen can show an interstitial.
  • After you complete a level — pressing Next Level can show an interstitial on every second completed level.

To keep ad density reasonable, at most one interstitial is shown in any 90-second period, and two are never shown back to back.

Rewarded video

Entirely optional and always initiated by you. Watching one reveals the maze again for three seconds, or grants an extra heart after a game over. No rewarded ad is forced, and none is triggered automatically.

What the ads SDK collects

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier for your device, used to make ads relevant and to measure ad performance
  • Device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, language, screen size, network type
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was requested, shown, viewed or clicked, and why an ad failed to load
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address for ad targeting, not from a device sensor

Google may use this data to personalise ads across apps and websites. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings, or through your Google account. See Google's partner site policy for details.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, call log or storage permission, and it shows no runtime permission prompt. There is no file picker and no photo access. All of the above are normal (not dangerous) permissions, so Android never asks the user to approve them.

Analytics, tracking and third parties

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no attribution or A/B testing framework, and no third-party trackers beyond the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We cannot see, log or retrieve any data about you, because we operate no servers and receive no data.

Children

Ages 13 and over

Memory Maze is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, we do not market it to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app serves advertisements, it is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families program and is not recommended for children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has used this app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where these laws apply to you, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or request portability.

In practice: we hold no personal data about you. Data held by Google through the ads SDK is subject to Google's privacy policy, and you can exercise your rights with Google directly via the links above. Progress stored by the app can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, by uninstalling, or by removing the app from your Google Drive backup.

Security

All app-to-network communication uses HTTPS, as enforced by the Android platform and the ads SDK. We do not transmit or store any personal data, so there is no user database to compromise.

Children's data in the Google Play system

This app is not in the Google Play "Designed for Families" program, and its target audience on Google Play is declared as ages 13 and over. It therefore does not request the Families Program self-certification, and it does not use interest-based or personalised advertising for children.
